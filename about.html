What is TaskFlow?

TaskFlow is a lightweight task-management web application designed to help individuals and teams organize their daily activities efficiently.

How to Use TaskFlow (Usage Guide)

  • Home / Dashboard: View your task summary.
  • Add Task: Create new tasks with due dates and priorities.
  • Task List: Filter and view all active or completed tasks.
  • Task Detail: View full details, mark complete, or delete.
